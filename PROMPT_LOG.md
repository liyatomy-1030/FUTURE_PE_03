# Prompt Log - FUTURE_PE_03

## Objective

Create an SEO blog and content cluster system for a local business website using structured prompt engineering.

## Prompt Engineering Decisions

- The model was assigned the role of SEO content strategist.
- Keyword intent was mapped before writing the blog.
- The outline was generated separately to ensure proper H1, H2, and H3 structure.
- Local SEO references were included to make the content relevant for Kochi searches.
- Internal links were planned to connect the blog to service pages and related content.
- Medical overclaiming was avoided by keeping the tone educational and appointment-focused.

## Reusable Master Prompt

```text
Act as an SEO content strategist and blog writer for local business websites.

Business details:
- Business name:
- Business type:
- Location:
- Main service:
- Target audience:
- SEO goal:

Create:
1. Keyword and search intent map
2. SEO title and meta description
3. Blog outline with H1, H2, and H3 structure
4. Long-form blog post
5. FAQ section
6. Content cluster with 8 supporting topics
7. Internal linking suggestions
8. Appointment-focused CTA

Rules:
- Use the primary keyword naturally.
- Make the content helpful and readable.
- Include local SEO context.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Write for real customers, not only search engines.
```

## Improvement Notes

Separating the keyword map, outline, and full blog helped produce a more organized SEO asset. The content cluster made the final output more useful for a real business website because it showed how one blog could support a larger content strategy.
